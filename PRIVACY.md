# Privacy policy for Cadence

Cadence uses the microphone only while you press Start.

Audio is processed on your phone. It is never uploaded or shared. The app never saves audio.

Cadence has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no network permission.

The optional field notes file is stored on your phone. It contains settings and sound levels only, no audio. It leaves your phone only if you copy it off yourself.

Contact: the issues page of this project, https://github.com/adamcseresznye/cadence/issues
