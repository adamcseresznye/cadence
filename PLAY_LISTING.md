# Play Store listing

## App name

Cadence

## Short description

Hear your own voice with a short delay to practice speech.

## Full description

Cadence plays your own voice back to you with a short delay. Speech practice uses this kind of delayed feedback.

What it does

Cadence listens to your microphone, delays your voice by 40 to 200 milliseconds, and plays it back to your earbuds. A gate mutes the playback when you are not speaking, so traffic and other background noise are not played back. You choose the delay and the sensitivity.

What you need

Wired earbuds with a microphone. Use earbuds, never the phone speaker, because the speaker can cause feedback. Keep the volume low.

How to start

1. Plug in wired earbuds. Cadence shows that the earbuds are connected.
2. Choose the delay and the sensitivity.
3. Press Start. Cadence keeps running while the screen is off and shows a notification. Press Stop to end it.

The sensitivity setting

A higher sensitivity opens the gate more easily, but it may let noise through. A lower sensitivity opens the gate only for clear speech.

Privacy

Audio is processed on your phone and is never uploaded or shared. Cadence has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no internet access.

Medical disclaimer

Cadence is a practice tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. For speech therapy, talk to a qualified speech-language therapist.
